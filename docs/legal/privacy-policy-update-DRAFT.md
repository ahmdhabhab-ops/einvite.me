## Changes to existing sections

### "Information we collect": add two items

- **Connected AI assistants:** if you connect your eInvite.me account to an AI assistant such as ChatGPT, we keep a record of that connection (which app it is, when it was made, and a sign-in token stored in a scrambled form) so the assistant can act for you until you disconnect it.
- **Photos for AI Bridal Studio:** if you use AI Bridal Studio, you upload a photo of yourself and a photo of a dress, and we create a preview image of you wearing the dress.

### "Information we collect": change the guest information item

Add at the end: "We also keep a history of every reply (who replied, their answer, how many are coming, when, and how the reply arrived), so hosts can always see what their guests answered."

### "Who we share it with": add to the service providers paragraph

- **fal.ai:** if you use AI Bridal Studio, your photo and the dress photo are sent to fal.ai, an AI image service, only to create your preview.
- **OpenAI (ChatGPT):** if you connect eInvite.me to ChatGPT, the information you ask ChatGPT to show or change is sent to ChatGPT. That covers your invitation's names, date, places and language, and a summary of your guests' replies with family names. ChatGPT's use of it is governed by OpenAI's own terms and privacy policy.

### "How long we keep it": add

- **AI Bridal Studio:** we don't store your original photo or the dress photo you upload. We keep each preview for **[30]** days, then delete it automatically. You can delete it earlier at any time from AI Bridal Studio. fal.ai processes the photos under its own terms and data retention.
- **ChatGPT connection:** a connection stays active until you disconnect it in ChatGPT or ask us to remove it. Sign-in tokens expire on their own: after 1 hour, or 30 days for renewal tokens.
- **Reply history:** kept for as long as the invitation is kept.

## New section: "Using eInvite.me from ChatGPT"

You can connect your eInvite.me account to ChatGPT (the eInvite.me plugin). When you do:

- **How you connect:** you sign in on eInvite.me's own page and choose what to allow. We never share your eInvite.me password with ChatGPT or OpenAI.
- **What ChatGPT can do, only for your own account:**
  - list your invitation;
  - create an invitation as a draft;
  - change the names, date, time, places or intro text of a draft;
  - show a summary of your guests' replies: numbers and family names, without phone numbers.
- **What it can't do:** send messages to your guests, publish an invitation, delete anything, make payments, or see other people's invitations.
- **What goes to OpenAI:** the information ChatGPT reads or changes through the plugin. OpenAI's own terms and privacy policy apply to it.
- **How to disconnect:** in ChatGPT's settings at any time, or contact us at **[contact email]** and we'll remove the connection.

## New section: "AI Bridal Studio"

AI Bridal Studio lets you see an AI-made preview of yourself in a dress.

- **What we ask for:** a clear photo of you and a photo of the dress (yours, or one from a dress shop listed on our site).
- **Who processes the photos:** to make the preview, both photos are sent to fal.ai, an AI image service. We ask for your agreement before every preview.
- **What we keep:**
  - we don't keep your original photo or the dress photo you upload;
  - the preview is private to your account, and only you can see or download it;
  - it is deleted automatically after **[30]** days, or when you delete it.
- **What a preview is:** an AI visual preview. It is not a measurement and doesn't guarantee how a real dress will fit or look.
- **Dress shops:** when you try a shop's dress, the shop's own site and terms apply to anything you buy there. We don't share your photos or previews with the shop.
- **Photos of other people:** please only upload photos of yourself, or of someone who has agreed to it.
